This Privacy Policy explains what information Affilio ("the App", "we") collects, why, and how it's handled. Affilio is a personal affiliate-link and earnings tracker. It is a template policy — replace the bracketed placeholders with your own details before submitting to the Play Store.

Information we collect

  • Account information: when you sign in with Google or email/password, we receive your email address, display name, and profile photo (Google only) from Firebase Authentication.
  • Content you create: affiliate program tracking IDs, the product links and captions you generate, and the click/order/commission figures you manually log.
  • Preferences: display currency and theme.
  • We do not collect location data, contacts, camera/microphone data, or browsing history outside the app.

How we use it

  • To sync your links, earnings history, and settings across your devices.
  • To authenticate you and keep your data private to your account.
  • To generate the affiliate links, share captions, and QR codes you request.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it's stored

Your data is stored in Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), operated under Google's infrastructure and security practices. A local, offline-capable cache is also kept on your device so the app works without a connection; it syncs once you're back online.

Third-party services

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication and database. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • open.er-api.com — used only to fetch public currency exchange rates; no personal data is sent to this service.
  • Links you create point to third-party affiliate networks (e.g. Amazon Associates, Flipkart) — their own privacy policies apply once a shopper clicks through.

Data retention & deletion

Your data is retained until you delete it. You can export your data at any time from Settings → Export data, and you can request account and data deletion by contacting us at [SUPPORT EMAIL].

Children's privacy

Affilio is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date above.
